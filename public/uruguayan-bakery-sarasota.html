Baked here, every day

The bakery — alfajores, roscas and breads

Valeria bakes. The press asked her what Sarasota was missing, and her answer was the pastry counter of her hometown — “delicious coffees” alongside it. Here is what that counter carries.

Alfajores de chocolate

“Grandes y chicos, hechos con mucho amor” — big ones and small, made with a lot of love, their own words. Two soft cookies sandwiching dulce de leche under a chocolate coat: the dessert counter of every Uruguayan panadería, at 7321 N Tamiami Trail.

Rosca de chicharrones

A ring bread worked through with chicharrones, baked fresh — the shop posts it listas a las 9:00 a.m., ready mid-morning. Come before it’s gone.

Budines

Pound cakes on the counter — Cake d’Oro among them, the one the shop recently ran three-for-$12 as its weekend deal.

Uruguayan breads

The breads to go with the coffee, baked in the shop — what Valeria told Business Debut she felt customers in Sarasota had not seen yet.

The bakes rotate week to week — today’s oven is announced on Instagram and Facebook.
