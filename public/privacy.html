Privacy

What this site does

This is the café’s own website: static pages, one contact form, and an embedded Google Map. It runs no analytics, no advertising trackers and no third-party scripts, and it sets no cookies of its own. The embedded map may set cookies under Google’s own policy once you interact with it.

The contact form

What you type — name, phone, email, message — is delivered to Two Shores Café’s inbox (Info@twoshorescafe.com) for the sole purpose of answering you. It is not added to any list, sold, or shared. Ask and we’ll confirm what we hold.

Questions

Call the café at (941) 298-9252, or write Info@twoshorescafe.com and the counter will answer.
