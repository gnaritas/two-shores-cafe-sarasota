“Hoy prendemos la parrilla”

Grill days — lomito, chivito and choripán

On announced days the café fires the parrilla and builds the sandwiches of the Río de la Plata to order — the food both Uruguay and Argentina stand on, sandwiched and eaten standing up at the counter.

Chivito al pan

Uruguay’s everything sandwich — steak and the works, in bread. The shop’s posts call it by name; when the grill is lit, it’s built to order.

Lomito

The neighbor sandwich: tender beef lomito, grilled and sandwiched recién hecho. Posted the morning it happens.

Choripán

Chorizo straight off the grill into bread — the stall classic of both shores of the river, chorizo included in the shop’s grill-day posts.

Milanesa de carne

Breaded beef, thin and fried, the lunch counter of Montevideo and Buenos Aires alike — on the grill-day card.

The grill lights on posted days — watch Instagram or Facebook that morning, or call the counter.
